Celery Potato Lemon Soup
------------------------

Ingredients
~~~~~~~~~~~

* 5 celery stalks (can also add some leaves)
* 3 medium potatoes
* 4 garlic cloves, chopped (not crushed)
* Juice from one whole lemon
* 1 tablespoon olive oil
* 1 teaspoon salt

Method
~~~~~~

1. Slice the celery into rings about 1/4 cm (1/10 inch) thick.
2. Peel and cut the potatoes into 2 cm (3/4 inch) cubes.
3. Heat olive oil in a pot and sauté the garlic, celery, and potatoes for about a minute.
4. Add 1 liter (4 cups) of water and salt.
5. Cook until the potatoes are done.
6. Add the lemon juice and cook for another 5 minutes.
7. Add more salt if needed.
8. Serve with white rice.
