Frozen Burger Patties in an Air Fryer Oven
==========================================

Frozen burger patties cooked straight from the freezer in a countertop oven
on its Air Fry setting.

Ingredients
-----------

* 450 grams frozen burger patties (4 patties of about 113 grams each)
* 5 grams oil, for brushing or spraying
* 2 grams salt
* 1 gram black pepper
* 80 grams sliced cheese (4 slices), optional

Preparation
-----------

1. **Preheat.** Set the oven to Air Fry at 400°F (200°C) with the basket
   inside, and wait until it reaches temperature. A countertop oven needs
   this more than a real air fryer does.
2. **Prep the patties.** Take them straight from the freezer without
   thawing. Brush or spray both sides with the 5 grams of oil and season
   with the 2 grams of salt and 1 gram of pepper.
3. **Load the basket.** Lay the patties in a single layer with space
   between them, on the rack position closest to the fan or upper element.
   Put a foil-lined tray on the rack below to catch drips.
4. **First side.** Cook for about 9–10 minutes.
5. **Flip.** Turn the patties over and cook another 7–10 minutes. This puts
   you in the 16–18 minute range at 400°F, and a weaker countertop fan can
   push it toward the 20–22 minutes another recipe gives for frozen
   patties.
6. **Check the center.** Use a thermometer pushed into the middle from the
   side. They're done at 160°F (71°C), and color won't tell you, because
   fully cooked patties can still look pink inside.
7. **If the outside is done but the middle isn't,** drop to 350°F (180°C)
   and give it 3–4 more minutes. That lets the center catch up without
   burning the crust.
8. **Cheese (optional).** Add a slice (about 20 grams) to each patty when
   the patties are around 155°F (68°C) and leave them in for a minute.

Notes
-----

* Once you've done one batch, write down the total time that worked. Every
  oven differs, and your second batch will be guesswork-free.
* The patty weight (quarter-pound, about 113 grams each), the amounts of
  oil, salt and pepper, and the cheese slice weight were not in the
  original instructions and are assumed. Adjust the count to fit your
  basket in a single layer.
