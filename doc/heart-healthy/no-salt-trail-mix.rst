No-Salt Trail Mix
=================

A satisfying, heart-healthy snack mix with no added sodium.

Ingredients
-----------

(Total ~530g)

* 240g walnuts
* 170g almonds
* 120g raisins

Method
------

1. Combine all ingredients in a large bowl
2. Mix thoroughly but gently to avoid breaking the nuts
3. Store in an airtight container

Notes
-----

* The walnuts and almonds provide healthy fats and protein
* Natural sweetness from raisins satisfies sweet cravings while adding potassium
* No added sodium
* Store at room temperature for up to 2 weeks
* As an added snack, eat pear, apple, or mandarin orange
