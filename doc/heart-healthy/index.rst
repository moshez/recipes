Heart-Healthy Recipes
=====================

A comprehensive collection of cardiac-friendly recipes with no added salt, whole carbohydrates, and heart-healthy ingredients.

All recipes in this collection share these heart-healthy characteristics:

- **No added salt** (or minimal, divided among servings)
- **Whole carbohydrates with fiber**
- **No caffeine**
- **Heart-healthy fats** from olive oil
- **Plant-based proteins** from beans, lentils, nutritional yeast
- **Rich in potassium** from vegetables
- **Gluten-free options** available (chickpea pasta, brown rice)
