White Rice (3 Cups Uncooked)
============================

Ingredients
-----------

* 555g uncooked long-grain white rice
* 1100g water
* 5g salt (optional)

Method
------

* Bring water and salt to a rolling boil.
* Add rice, stir once to prevent sticking.
* Return to boil, then reduce to 250F on induction stove and cover tightly.
* Cook 15–17 minutes.
* Remove from heat, keep covered, rest 5–10 minutes.
* Fluff with a fork and serve.

Notes
-----

**Lid seal:** If your lid doesn't seal well, reduce water by about 50g.

**Altitude:** At high altitude, add 50–100g extra water and a couple
extra minutes of cooking time.

**Resting:** Don't skip it—the resting period redistributes moisture
and prevents a wet top layer with crunchy bottom.
